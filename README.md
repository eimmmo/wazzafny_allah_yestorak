# Wazzafny (Hire Me Please!)

Most people build To-Do apps to show off their Flutter skills. I built a digital cry for help. 

## What is this?
It's a high-stakes dice game that perfectly simulates my job hunt:
- **Intro:** A video politely begging: *"Can you hire me pleeeeease?"*
- **Roll ≥ 10:** You win! The app blesses you with a happy video and an *"Allah yestorak"*.
- **Roll < 10:** You lose. You get a sad video and a heavy *"Mennak lellah"*.

## Built With
- **Flutter & Dart**
- **`video_player`** (to seamlessly stream my emotions)
- **`dart:math`** (because the job market is completely random anyway)
- **100% Pure Desperation**

## How to Run
Clone the repo, run `flutter run`, and test your luck.
*(Disclaimer: If you roll a 10, you are legally obligated to send me a job offer).*
